# haunted-mansion
made using javascript, html/css


An interactive comic about a crew of thieves who break into Blackwood Manor looking for a legendary treasure, only to meet the ghost who still lives there.


## Story
You and your crew (Marcus, Lena, Briggs) sneak into Blackwood Manor to find
"The Heart of Blackwood." A chandelier crash separates you from the others,
and you're left to explore the mansion alone. Along the way you meet Evelyn,
the ghost of the house, uncover clues about what really happened to the
Blackwood family, and ultimately choose how the heist ends.

## How to play
- Click through the intro to enter the mansion.
- Click interactive objects (blueprint, tablet, coin, portrait) to read clues and unlock the path forward.
- Talk to Evelyn
- Search the three rooms (Kitchen, Study, Ballroom) to collect evidence.
- Reach the vault and make a final choice: help Marcus take the treasure, or return the keepsakes.
- Fully explore every object, question, and room to unlock a hidden epilogue.

## Features
- Branching dialogue with a typewriter text effect
- Inventory system that tracks collected clues
- Scene-based progress indicator
- Two distinct endings plus a secret epilogue for full exploration
- Pixel/retro-styled UI with hover and click animations

## Files
- `index.html` — structure and content
- `styles.css` — all visual styling
- `script.js` — game state, scene logic, and interactivity
